    About Ovenbird

    Ovenbird is the product of the 3D concrete printing research and the gradient multi-material extrusion research at the Polyhedral Structures Laboratory (PSL), which is part of the Weitzman School of Design at the University of Pennsylvania.

    The plug-in is named after the red ovenbird (Furnarius rufus, also known as Rufous Hornero), a South American bird that builds shell nests by gradually depositing mud, similar to the process of 3D concrete printing. We share ovenbirds' vision of printing our own houses.
